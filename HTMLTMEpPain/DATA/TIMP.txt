1  4180 150 MERCURY EXPOSURE INDUCES CYTOSKELETON DISRUPTION AND LOSS OF RENAL FUNCTION THROUGH EPIGENETIC MODULATION OF MMP9 EXPRESSION. ANIMAL AND EPIDEMIOLOGICAL STUDIES INDICATE THAT MEHG AFFECTS NEURAL AND RENAL FUNCTION. OUR STUDY IS FOCUSED ON NEPHROTOXIC POTENTIAL OF MEHG. IN THIS STUDY, WE HAVE SHOWN FOR THE FIRST TIME HOW MEHG COULD EPIGENETICALLY MODULATE MATRIX METALLOPROTEINASE 9(MMP9) TO PROMOTE NEPHROTOXICITY USING AN ANIMAL MODEL OF SUB CHRONIC MEHG EXPOSURE.
