DETECTION OF PROMOTER HYPERMETHYLATION OF TUMOR SUPPRESSOR GENES IN EXFOLIATED CELLS FROM THE LUNG PROVIDES AN ASSESSMENT OF FIELD CANCERIZATION THAT IN TURN PREDICTS LUNG CANCER. THE ASSOCIATIONS COULD BE MEDIATED BY GABAERGIC SIGNALING THAT DRIVES THE SMOKING-INDUCED MUCOUS CELL METAPLASIA.
