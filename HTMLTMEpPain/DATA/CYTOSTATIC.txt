CONCLUSIONS: COLLECTIVELY, THE PRESENT FINDINGS SHOW THAT COMBINATIONS HDAC INHIBITOR-IMATINIB ARE LIKELY TO OVERCOME DRUG RESISTANCE IN CML PATHOLOGY.	2020

4  2402  58 EPIGENETIC REPROGRAMMING SENSITIZES CML STEM CELLS TO COMBINED EZH2 AND TYROSINE KINASE INHIBITION. A MAJOR OBSTACLE TO CURING CHRONIC MYELOID LEUKEMIA (CML) IS RESIDUAL DISEASE MAINTAINED BY TYROSINE KINASE INHIBITOR (TKI)-PERSISTENT LEUKEMIC STEM CELLS (LSC). WE DEMONSTRATE THAT EZH2 AND H3K27ME3 REPROGRAMMING IS IMPORTANT FOR LSC SURVIVAL, BUT RENDERS LSCS SENSITIVE TO THE COMBINED EFFECTS OF EZH2I AND TKI.
