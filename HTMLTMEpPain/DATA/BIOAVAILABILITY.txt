KEY BENEFITS OF THESE AGENTS STEM FROM THEIR ABILITY TO TARGET MULTIPLE PHYSIOLOGICAL AREAS VIA THE GUT BRAIN-AXIS AND ARE DEVOID OF POTENTIAL HARMFUL OR AGGRAVATING EFFECTS ON ASD PATIENTS.
