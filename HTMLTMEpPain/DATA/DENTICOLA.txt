7  2223  25 EPIGENETIC MODIFICATIONS IN SPINAL LIGAMENT AGING. SPINAL STENOSIS IS A COMMON DEGENERATIVE SPINE DISORDER IN THE AGED POPULATION AND THE SPINAL LIGAMENT AGING IS A MAIN CONTRIBUTOR TO THIS CHRONIC DISEASE. HOWEVER, THE UNDERLYING MECHANISMS OF SPINAL LIGAMENT AGING REMAIN UNCLEAR. EPIGENETICS IS THE STUDY OF HERITABLE AND REVERSIBLE CHANGES IN THE FUNCTION OF A GENE OR GENOME THAT OCCUR WITHOUT ANY ALTERATION IN THE PRIMARY DNA SEQUENCE. EPIGENETIC ALTERATIONS HAVE BEEN DEMONSTRATED TO PLAY CRUCIAL ROLES IN AGE-RELATED DISEASES AND CONDITIONS, AND THEY ARE RECENTLY STUDIED AS BIOMARKERS AND THERAPEUTIC TARGETS IN THE FIELD OF CANCER RESEARCH. HISTONE DEACETYLASES (HDACS) ARE IMPORTANT REGULATORS OF GENE EXPRESSION THAT ARE ABERRANTLY REGULATED IN SEVERAL INFLAMMATORY AND INFECTIOUS DISEASES.
