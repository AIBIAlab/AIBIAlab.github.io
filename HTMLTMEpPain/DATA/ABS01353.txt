FURTHERMORE, CERTAIN RADICAL SCAVENGERS SUPPRESSED THE INVASIVE PHENOTYPE OF THE CELLS. THESE RESULTS INDICATE THAT PERIGENETIC ALTERATIONS ARE AN IMPORTANT TARGET OF PHARMACOLOGICAL INTERVENTION OF CARCINOGENESIS.	2003
