NON-ALCOHOLIC FATTY LIVER DISEASE (NAFLD), AN ACCUMULATION OF INTRA-HEPATIC TRIGLYCERIDES THAT IS OFTEN CONSIDERED THE HEPATIC MANIFESTATION OF INSULIN RESISTANCE, IS THE MOST COMMON CAUSE OF CHRONIC LIVER DISEASE IN THE WESTERN COUNTRIES WITH UP TO ONE THIRD OF THE POPULATION AFFECTED.
