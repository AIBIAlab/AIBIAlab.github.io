14 6190  37 THE IMPACT OF METHYLATION QUANTITATIVE TRAIT LOCI (MQTLS) ON ACTIVE SMOKING-RELATED DNA METHYLATION CHANGES. BACKGROUND: METHYLATION QUANTITATIVE TRAIT LOCI (MQTLS) ARE THE GENETIC VARIANTS THAT MAY AFFECT THE DNA METHYLATION PATTERNS OF CPG SITES.
