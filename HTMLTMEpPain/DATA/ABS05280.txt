19 6252  29 THE MICROBIOME AND CANCER: IMPLICATIONS FOR ONCOLOGY NURSING SCIENCE. BACKGROUND: APPROXIMATELY 1.6 MILLION AMERICANS WERE DIAGNOSED WITH CANCER IN 2014. ALTERATIONS IN THE NORMAL FLORA OF THE GUT MAY ALSO INFLUENCE CANCER SYMPTOMS. METHODS: EXTANT LITERATURE WAS REVIEWED AND SYNTHESIZED. RESULTS: THE MAJORITY OF STUDIES LINKING THE GUT MICROBIOTA AND CANCER ARE ANIMAL MODELS AND FOCUS ON THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN DYSBIOSIS AND COLORECTAL CANCER.
