IN ADDITION, MANY SMALL MOLECULES SHOW PROFOUND THERAPEUTIC EFFECTS ON RIF BY SUPPRESSING THE ORIGIN AND ACTIVATION OF RENAL MYOFIBROBLASTS. TAKEN TOGETHER, THE REVIEW FOCUSES ON THE MECHANISMS OF THE ORIGIN AND ACTIVATION OF RENAL MYOFIBROBLASTS IN RIF AND THE SMALL MOLECULES AGAINST THEM IMPROVING RIF, WHICH WILL PROVIDE A NEW INSIGHT FOR RIF THERAPY.	2021
