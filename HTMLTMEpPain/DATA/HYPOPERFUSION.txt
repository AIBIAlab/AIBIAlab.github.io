CONCLUSIONS: EE WAS LIKELY TO BE SUPERIOR TO TET1 HIPPOCAMPAL ADMINISTRATION FOR COGNITION AND PSYCHIATRIC BEHAVIORS IN CCH RATS. FURTHERMORE, A HEALTHY LOCAL BRAIN ENVIRONMENT WITH ELEVATED BDNF AND ASTROCYTES WAS CONDUCIVE TO IMPROVING COGNITIVE DYSFUNCTION. MORE GLIAL ACTIVATION, AND MORE VULNERABLE TO DEPRESSIVE DISORDERS.
