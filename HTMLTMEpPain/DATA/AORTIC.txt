1  3656 145 INDUCIBLE PRMT1 ABLATION IN ADULT VASCULAR SMOOTH MUSCLE LEADS TO CONTRACTILE DYSFUNCTION AND AORTIC DISSECTION. VASCULAR SMOOTH MUSCLE CELLS (VSMCS) HAVE REMARKABLE PLASTICITY IN RESPONSE TO DIVERSE ENVIRONMENTAL CUES. ALTHOUGH THESE CELLS ARE VERSATILE, CHRONIC STRESS CAN TRIGGER VSMC DYSFUNCTION, WHICH ULTIMATELY LEADS TO VASCULAR DISEASES SUCH AS AORTIC ANEURYSM AND ATHEROSCLEROSIS. ALTHOUGH A POTENTIAL ROLE OF PRMT1 IN VASCULAR PATHOGENESIS HAS BEEN PROPOSED, ITS ROLE IN VASCULAR FUNCTION HAS YET TO BE CLARIFIED.
