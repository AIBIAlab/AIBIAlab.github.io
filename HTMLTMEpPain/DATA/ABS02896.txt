1 2896 130 GASTRIC ENTEROCHROMAFFIN-LIKE CELL HYPERPLASIA AND NEOPLASIA IN THE RAT: AN INDIRECT EFFECT OF THE HISTAMINE H2-RECEPTOR ANTAGONIST, BL-6341. ORAL ADMINISTRATION OF BL-6341 HYDROCHLORIDE, A LONG-ACTING HISTAMINE H2-RECEPTOR ANTAGONIST, TO RATS FOR 2 YEARS AT DOSES OF 10, 55 OR 300 MG/KG/DAY RESULTED IN SEVERAL CHANGES IN THE FUNDIC (OXYNTIC) MUCOSA OF THE GLANDULAR STOMACH. THE REFERENCE COMPOUND, CIMETIDINE (950 MG/KG), PRODUCED A DEGREE OF ECL CELL PROLIFERATION THAT WAS SLIGHTLY LESS, BUT NOT SIGNIFICANTLY DIFFERENT THAN, THAT OBSERVED WITH 55 MG/KG OF BL-6341. IT WAS CONCLUDED THAT THE DEMONSTRATED HYPERGASTRINEMIA REPRESENTS AN INDIRECT, HORMONAL, EPIGENETIC MECHANISM OF TUMORIGENESIS.	1988	
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
2 3718  30 INHIBITION OF BCL6B PROMOTES GASTRIC CANCER BY AMPLIFYING INFLAMMATION IN MICE. BACKGROUND: CHRONIC GASTRITIS HAS BEEN DEMONSTRATED TO BE A KEY CAUSE OF GASTRIC CANCER (GC), AND CONTROL OF GASTRIC INFLAMMATION IS REGARDED AS AN EFFECTIVE TREATMENT FOR THE CLINICAL PREVENTION OF GASTRIC CARCINOGENESIS.
