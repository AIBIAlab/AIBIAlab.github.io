10  4149  32 MECHANISTIC INSIGHT INTO THE EFFECTS OF CURCUMIN ON NEUROINFLAMMATION-DRIVEN CHRONIC PAIN. CHRONIC PAIN IS A PERSISTENT AND UNREMITTING CONDITION THAT HAS IMMENSE EFFECTS ON PATIENTS' QUALITY OF LIFE. STUDIES HAVE SHOWN THAT NEUROINFLAMMATION IS ASSOCIATED WITH THE INDUCTION AND PROGRESSION OF CHRONIC PAIN. THE ACTIVATION OF MICROGLIA AND ASTROCYTES IS THE MAJOR HALLMARK OF SPINAL NEUROINFLAMMATION LEADING TO NEURONAL EXCITABILITY IN THE PROJECTION NEURONS. EXCESSIVE ACTIVATION OF MICROGLIA AND ASTROCYTES IS ONE OF THE MAJOR CONTRIBUTING FACTORS TO THE EXACERBATION OF PAIN.
