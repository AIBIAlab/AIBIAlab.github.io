CONCLUSIONS OUR FINDINGS SUGGEST THAT L-ACETYLCARNITINE HAS THE UNIQUE PROPERTY TO CAUSE A LONG-LASTING ANALGESIC EFFECT THAT MIGHT REDUCE RELAPSES IN PATIENTS SUFFERING FROM CHRONIC PAIN.	2017
