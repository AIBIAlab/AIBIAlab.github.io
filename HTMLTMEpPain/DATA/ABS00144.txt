6 5687  34 SIGNIFICANCE OF INACTIVATED GENES IN LEUKEMIA: PATHOGENESIS AND PROGNOSIS. 8  606  39 BEYOND GENETICS--THE EMERGING ROLE OF EPIGENETIC CHANGES IN HEMATOPOIETIC MALIGNANCIES. DNA METHYLATION AT CYTOSINE BASES THAT ARE LOCATED 5' TO GUANOSINE WITHIN A CPG DINUCLEOTIDE IS THE MAIN EPIGENETIC MODIFICATION IN HUMANS. PATTERNS OF DNA METHYLATION ARE PROFOUNDLY DERANGED IN HUMAN CANCER AND COMPRISE GENOME-WIDE LOSSES AS WELL AS REGIONAL GAINS IN DNA METHYLATION. A LARGE NUMBER OF GENES INVOLVING FUNDAMENTAL CELLULAR PATHWAYS MAY BE AFFECTED IN VIRTUALLY ALL TYPES OF HUMAN CANCER BY ABERRANT CPG ISLAND METHYLATION IN ASSOCIATION WITH TRANSCRIPTIONAL SILENCING.
