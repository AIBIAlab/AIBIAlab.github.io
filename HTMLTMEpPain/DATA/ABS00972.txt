CONCLUSIONS: IN SUM, WE FOUND THAT SMOKING-INDUCED DMRS HAVE DIFFERENT DISTRIBUTION PATTERNS IN HYPERMETHYLATED AND HYPOMETHYLATED AREAS BETWEEN SMOKERS AND NONSMOKERS. WE FURTHER IDENTIFIED AND VERIFIED SMOKING-RELATED DMGS AND DEGS THROUGH MULTI-OMICS INTEGRATION ANALYSIS OF DNA METHYLOME AND TRANSCRIPTOME DATA. THESE FINDINGS PROVIDE US A COMPREHENSIVE GENOMIC MAP OF THE MOLECULAR CHANGES INDUCED BY SMOKING WHICH WOULD ENHANCE OUR UNDERSTANDING OF THE HARMS OF SMOKING AND ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH DISEASES.	2021
