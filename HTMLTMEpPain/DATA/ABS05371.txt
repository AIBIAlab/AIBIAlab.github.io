14 3020  45 GENETICS AND EPIGENETICS OF OSTEOARTHRITIS. OSTEOARTHRITIS (OA) IS A COMMON AGE-RELATED DISEASE THAT AFFECTS THE TISSUES OF THE SYNOVIAL JOINT, LEADING TO LOSS OF FUNCTION AND PAIN. 16 6107  36 THE EMERGING ROLE OF NONCODING RNAS IN SYSTEMIC LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS: NEW INSIGHTS INTO THE MASTER REGULATORS OF DISEASE PATHOGENESIS. HENCE, CLARIFYING THE FUNCTION OF THESE GROUPS OF NCRNAS IN THE PATHOPHYSIOLOGY OF SLE PROVIDES A DEEPER UNDERSTANDING OF THE DISEASE.
