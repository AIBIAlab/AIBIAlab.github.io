1  6077 127 THE EFFECT OF CD4 RECEPTOR DOWNREGULATION AND ITS DOWNSTREAM SIGNALING MOLECULES ON HIV-1 LATENCY. HIV-1 CAN ESTABLISH A LATENT INFECTION IN MEMORY CD4+T CELLS TO EVADE THE HOST IMMUNE RESPONSE. CD4 MOLECULES CAN ACT NOT ONLY AS THE HIV-1 RECEPTOR FOR ENTRY BUT ALSO AS THE TRIGGER IN AN INTRACELLULAR SIGNALING CASCADE FOR T-CELL ACTIVATION AND PROLIFERATION VIA PROTEIN TYROSINE KINASES. NOVEL CHRONIC HIV-1-INFECTED A3.01-DERIVED (NCHA) CELLS WERE USED TO EXAMINE THE INVOLVEMENT OF CD4 DOWNSTREAM SIGNALING IN HIV-1 LATENCY.
