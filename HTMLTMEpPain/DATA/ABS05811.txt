INDEED, EPIGENETIC MODIFIERS THAT ARE MUTATED IN CHILDREN WITH GENETIC NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISEASES ARE REGULATED BY HYPOXIA IN A NUMBER OF PRECLINICAL MODELS AND MAY BE PART OF THE MECHANISM FOR THE LONG-TERM NEURODEVELOPMENTAL SEQUELAE SEEM IN CHILDREN WITH HYPOXIC BRAIN INJURY.
