THIS COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS SUPPORTS THE CONCEPT THAT THE EPIGENETIC CHANGES ALONG WITH THE ALTERED EXPRESSION OF GENES HAVE THE POTENTIAL TO PREDICT CLINICAL OUTCOME IN EARLY STAGE CLL PATIENTS.	2017
