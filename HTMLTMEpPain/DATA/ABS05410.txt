EPIGENETIC MODIFICATIONS, INCLUDING CYTOSINE DNA METHYLATION AND COVALENT POST-TRANSLATIONAL MODIFICATIONS OF HISTONES IN CHROMATIN, ARE PART OF THE EPIGENOME, THE INTERFACE BETWEEN THE STABLE GENOME AND THE VARIABLE ENVIRONMENT.
