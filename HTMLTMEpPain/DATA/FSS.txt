11  3462  35 HYPOTHALAMIC NR3C1 DNA METHYLATION IN RATS EXPOSED TO PRENATAL STRESS. BACKGROUND: HUMAN AND ANIMAL STUDIES HAVE INDICATED THAT MATERNAL PRENATAL STRESS (PS) HAS MOLECULAR AND BEHAVIORAL EFFECTS DURING PREGNANCY AND EARLY LIFE. THE PRESENT STUDY AIMED TO EVALUATE THE EPIGENETIC CHANGES OF THE NR3C1 GENE INVOLVED IN THE HPA AXIS IN THE HYPOTHALAMIC TISSUES OF RATS EXPOSED TO PS INDUCED BY CHRONIC UNPREDICTABLE MILD STRESS (CUMS).
