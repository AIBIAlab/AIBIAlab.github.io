16 1070  24 CLONAL ARCHITECTURE OF CHRONIC MYELOMONOCYTIC LEUKEMIAS. 17 4485  32 MOLECULAR SIMILARITY BETWEEN MYELODYSPLASTIC FORM OF CHRONIC MYELOMONOCYTIC LEUKEMIA AND REFRACTORY ANEMIA WITH RING SIDEROBLASTS. CHRONIC MYELOMONOCYTIC LEUKEMIA IS SIMILAR TO BUT A SEPARATE ENTITY FROM BOTH MYELOPROLIFERATIVE NEOPLASMS AND MYELODYSPLASTIC SYNDROMES, AND SHOWS EITHER MYELOPROLIFERATIVE OR MYELODYSPLASTIC FEATURES.
