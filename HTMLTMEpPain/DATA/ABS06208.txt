1 6208 157 THE INTERACTION BETWEEN MICRORNA-152 AND DNA METHYLTRANSFERASE-1 AS AN EPIGENETIC PROGNOSTIC BIOMARKER IN HCV-INDUCED LIVER CIRRHOSIS AND HCC PATIENTS.
