10 6257  31 THE MOLECULAR BASIS OF TOLERANCE. TOLERANCE IS DEFINED AS THE DIMINISHED RESPONSE TO ALCOHOL OR OTHER DRUGS OVER THE COURSE OF REPEATED OR PROLONGED EXPOSURE.
