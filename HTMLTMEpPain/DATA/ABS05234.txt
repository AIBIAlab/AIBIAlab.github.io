THE DIFFERENTIAL EXPRESSION OF MIR-145A-5P AND NR4A2 WAS FURTHER CONFIRMED IN PATIENTS WITH NASH, RAISING THE POSSIBILITY THAT SUPPLEMENTATION OF MIR-145A-5P OR SUPPRESSION OF NR4A2 IN HEPATOCYTES MIGHT PROVIDE NOVEL STRATEGIES FOR TREATING NASH.	2023
