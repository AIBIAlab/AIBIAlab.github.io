FINALLY, IT IS EMERGING THAT CHANGES IN HPA AXIS FUNCTION AND BEHAVIOR FOLLOWING ANTENATAL EXPOSURE TO SGC ARE TRANSGENERATIONAL AND LIKELY INVOLVE EPIGENETIC MECHANISMS. A COMPREHENSIVE UNDERSTANDING OF THE ACUTE AND LONG-TERM IMPACT OF SGC EXPOSURE IN UTERO IS NECESSARY TO BEGIN TO DEVELOP RECOMMENDATIONS AND TREATMENT OPTIONS FOR PREGNANT WOMEN AT RISK OF PRETERM DELIVERY.	2008
