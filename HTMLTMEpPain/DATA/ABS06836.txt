1 6836 135 [INFLUENCE OF METFORMIN ON THE DIAMETER AND NUMBER OF DNA BREAKS IN BLOOD LYMPHOCYTES IN OBESITY]. AIM - TO ASSESS THE DNA DAMAGE OF LYMPHOCYTES BEFORE AND AFTER THE USE OF METFORMIN IN OBESE INDIVIDUALS BY TWO INDICATORS: THE DIAMETER AND THE NUMBER OF DNA BREAKS IN BLOOD LYMPHOCYTES.
