1 3462 122 HYPOTHALAMIC NR3C1 DNA METHYLATION IN RATS EXPOSED TO PRENATAL STRESS. THE PRESENT STUDY AIMED TO EVALUATE THE EPIGENETIC CHANGES OF THE NR3C1 GENE INVOLVED IN THE HPA AXIS IN THE HYPOTHALAMIC TISSUES OF RATS EXPOSED TO PS INDUCED BY CHRONIC UNPREDICTABLE MILD STRESS (CUMS). 8  578  31 BEHAVIOR, BDNF AND EPIGENETIC MECHANISMS IN RESPONSE TO SOCIAL ISOLATION AND SOCIAL SUPPORT IN MIDDLE AGED RATS EXPOSED TO CHRONIC STRESS.
