CONCLUSION: WE FOUND MULTIPLE DIFFERENTIALLY METHYLATED CPG SITES ASSOCIATED WITH CHRONIC LUNG DISEASE. THESE SIGNALS COULD CONTRIBUTE TO BETTER UNDERSTAND MOLECULAR MECHANISMS INVOLVED IN LUNG DISEASE, AS ASSESSED SYSTEMICALLY, AS WELL AS TO IDENTIFY PATTERNS THAT COULD BE USEFUL FOR DIAGNOSTIC PURPOSES.
