HERE, WE SHOW THAT THE BROMODOMAIN PROTEIN TGBDP1 IS ESSENTIAL FOR PARASITE SURVIVAL AND THAT LOSS OF TGBDP1 RESULTS IN GLOBAL DYSREGULATION OF GENE EXPRESSION. TGBDP1 IS RECRUITED TO THE PROMOTER REGION OF A LARGE PROPORTION OF PARASITE GENES, FORMS A CORE COMPLEX WITH TWO OTHER BROMODOMAIN PROTEINS, AND INTERACTS WITH DIFFERENT TRANSCRIPTIONAL REGULATORY COMPLEXES.
