4   101  36 A RAT METHYL-SEQ PLATFORM TO IDENTIFY EPIGENETIC CHANGES ASSOCIATED WITH STRESS EXPOSURE. THE RAT DESIGN TARGETED PROMOTERS, CPG ISLANDS, ISLAND SHORES, AND GC-RICH REGIONS FROM ALL REFSEQ GENES. TOP CANDIDATE DMRS WERE INDEPENDENTLY VALIDATED BY BISULFITE PYROSEQUENCING TO CONFIRM THE ROBUSTNESS OF THE PLATFORM. RESULTS DEMONSTRATE THAT THE RAT METHYL-SEQ PLATFORM IS A USEFUL EPIGENETIC TOOL THAT CAN CAPTURE METHYLATION CHANGES INDUCED BY EXPOSURE TO STRESS.	2018
