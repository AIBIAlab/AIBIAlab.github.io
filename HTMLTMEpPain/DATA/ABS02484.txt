THIS AQUATIC CRUSTACEAN IS SENSITIVE TO ENVIRONMENTAL STRESSORS AND DISPLAYS CONSIDERABLE PLASTICITY IN ADAPTING TO CHANGING ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS.
