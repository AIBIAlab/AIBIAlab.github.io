20   296  28 AGING, CELL SENESCENCE, THE PATHOGENESIS AND TARGETED THERAPIES OF OSTEOARTHRITIS. AGING IS A KEY FACTOR IN ITS DEVELOPMENT. CURRENT TREATMENTS ARE PALLIATIVE AND NO DISEASE MODIFYING ANTI-OSTEOARTHRITIS DRUGS (DMOADS) ARE AVAILABLE.
