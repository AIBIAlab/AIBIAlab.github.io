20  5266  33 PROMOTED INTERACTION OF C/EBPALPHA WITH DEMETHYLATED CXCR3 GENE PROMOTER CONTRIBUTES TO NEUROPATHIC PAIN IN MICE. DNA METHYLATION HAS BEEN IMPLICATED IN THE PATHOGENESIS OF CHRONIC PAIN. OUR STUDY REVEALS AN EPIGENETIC MECHANISM UNDERLYING CXCR3 EXPRESSION AND ALSO SUGGESTS THAT TARGETING THE EXPRESSION OR ACTIVATION OF CXCR3 SIGNALING MAY OFFER NEW THERAPEUTICS FOR NEUROPATHIC PAIN.	2017
