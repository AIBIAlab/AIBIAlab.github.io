PRENATAL FACTORS THAT INCLUDE MATERNAL AND PATERNAL INFLUENCES ON THE OFFSPRING, AND POSTNATAL FACTORS, RANGING FROM BIRTH THROUGH CHILDHOOD, ADOLESCENCE AND YOUNG ADULTHOOD, AS WELL AS INTER-GENERATIONAL INFLUENCES HAVE BEEN EXPLORED USING THE LIFE COURSE APPROACH TO CHRONIC DISEASE.
