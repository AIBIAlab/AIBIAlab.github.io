19 1898  23 ENDOTHELINS IN CARDIOVASCULAR BIOLOGY AND THERAPEUTICS. ENDOTHELIN 1 (ET-1) AND ET-2 ACTIVATE TWO G PROTEIN-COUPLED RECEPTORS - ENDOTHELIN RECEPTOR TYPE A (ET(A)) AND ENDOTHELIN RECEPTOR TYPE B (ET(B)) - WITH EQUAL AFFINITY, WHEREAS ET-3 HAS A LOWER AFFINITY FOR ET(A).
