1  3203 139 HDAC3 ACTIVITY WITHIN THE NUCLEUS ACCUMBENS REGULATES COCAINE-INDUCED PLASTICITY AND BEHAVIOR IN A CELL-TYPE-SPECIFIC MANNER. DESPITE THIS, IT REMAINS UNKNOWN HOW COCAINE ALTERS HDAC3-DEPENDENT MECHANISMS. HERE, WE PROFILED HDAC3 EXPRESSION AND ACTIVITY IN TOTAL NAC MOUSE TISSUE FOLLOWING COCAINE EXPOSURE. ALTHOUGH CHRONIC COCAINE DID NOT AFFECT EXPRESSION OF HDAC3 WITHIN THE NAC, CHRONIC COCAINE DID AFFECT PROMOTER-SPECIFIC CHANGES IN HDAC3 AND H4K8AC OCCUPANCY.
