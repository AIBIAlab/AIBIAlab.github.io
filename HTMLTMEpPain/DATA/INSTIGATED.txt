EPIGENETIC CHANGES COEXISTED WITH ALTERED EXPRESSION OF ADIPOKINES, ESPECIALLY FOR IL-6; THEREFORE, EPIGENETIC MARKS INDUCED BY TRANSIENT HG MAY ACT AS EPI-MEMORY IN ADS. THE OVERALL S-PHASE ABNORMALITIES OBSERVED LEAD TO UNDER-REPLICATED DNA IN G2/M THAT INSTIGATES MITOTIC DNA SYNTHESIS. WE DEMONSTRATE THAT THE DNA REPLICATION DYNAMICS OF EP300-MUTATED CELLS ATLL CELLS RECAPITULATE FEATURES OF BRCA-DEFICIENT CANCERS.
