IN THIS REVIEW, WE DISCUSSED THE ROLES OF NON-CODING RNAS, SPECIFICALLY MICRORNAS (MIRNAS) AND LONG NON-CODING RNAS (LNCRNAS), IN SUCH PROCESSES. WE FURTHER OUTLINED OTHER EPIGENETIC MECHANISMS REGULATING THE BIOGENESIS AND/OR FUNCTION OF NON-CODING RNAS. OVERALL, WE BELIEVE THAT BETTER UNDERSTANDING OF SUCH MOLECULAR PROCESSES MAY LEAD TO THE DEVELOPMENT OF NOVEL BIOMARKERS AND THERAPEUTIC STRATEGIES IN THE FUTURE.	2019
