IN THE LAST DECADE, TUMOR GENOMICS HAS CONTRIBUTED TO THE IMPROVEMENT OF OUR UNDERSTANDING OF TUMORIGENESIS AND HAS LED TO THE IDENTIFICATION OF NUMEROUS CELLULAR TARGETS FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF NOVEL THERAPIES.
