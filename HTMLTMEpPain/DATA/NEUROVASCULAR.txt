20  1869  27 EMERGING ROLE OF DREAM IN HEALTHY BRAIN AND NEUROLOGICAL DISEASES. THE DOWNSTREAM REGULATORY ELEMENT ANTAGONIST MODULATOR (DREAM) IS A MULTIFUNCTIONAL CA(2+)-SENSITIVE PROTEIN EXERTING A DUAL MECHANISM OF ACTION TO REGULATE SEVERAL CA(2+)-DEPENDENT PROCESSES. ON THE OTHER HAND, DREAM COULD ALSO DIRECTLY MODULATE THE ACTIVITY OR THE LOCALIZATION OF SEVERAL CYTOSOLIC AND PLASMA MEMBRANE PROTEINS. THESE FINDINGS LEAD TO THE CONCEPT THAT DREAM MIGHT REPRESENT A PHARMACOLOGICAL TARGET TO AMELIORATE SYMPTOMS AND REDUCE NEURODEGENERATIVE PROCESSES IN SEVERAL PATHOLOGICAL CONDITIONS AFFECTING CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM.	2023
