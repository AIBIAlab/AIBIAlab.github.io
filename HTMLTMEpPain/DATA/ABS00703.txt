13 2262  37 EPIGENETIC PROFILING IN CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA REVEALS NOVEL METHYLATION TARGETS. CPG ISLAND METHYLATION IS AN EPIGENETIC ALTERATION THAT CONTRIBUTES TO TUMORIGENESIS BY TRANSCRIPTIONAL INACTIVATION OF GENES.
