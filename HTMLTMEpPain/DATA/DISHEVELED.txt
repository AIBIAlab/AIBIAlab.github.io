1  1700 147 DYNAMIC EXPRESSION OF ZNF382 AND ITS TUMOR-SUPPRESSOR ROLE IN HEPATITIS B VIRUS-RELATED HEPATOCELLULAR CARCINOGENESIS. ZINC-FINGER PROTEIN 382 (ZNF382), WHICH BELONGS TO ZINC-FINGER PROTEIN FAMILY, HAS BEEN DOCUMENTED TO BE DOWNREGULATED IN CERTAIN TYPES OF CANCER. HOWEVER, ITS ROLE IN HCC REMAINS LARGELY UNKNOWN.
