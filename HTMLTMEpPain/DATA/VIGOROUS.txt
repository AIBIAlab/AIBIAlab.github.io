THIS EFFECT LIKELY OCCURS VIA DOWNREGULATION OF RIG-I AND TLR3 DUE TO SMOKE-INDUCED EPIGENETIC MODIFICATIONS. REDUCTION IN LUNG EPITHELIAL CELL RIG-I AND TLR3 RESPONSES MAY BE A MAJOR MECHANISM CONTRIBUTING TO THE INCREASED RISK AND SEVERITY OF VIRAL RESPIRATORY INFECTIONS IN SMOKERS AND TO VIRAL-MEDIATED ACUTE EXACERBATIONS OF COPD.	2016
