OSTEOARTHRITIS (OA) IS A CHRONIC DISEASE THAT AFFECTS ARTICULAR CARTILAGE, CAUSING ITS DEGENERATION. ALTHOUGH OA IS ONE OF THE MOST PREVALENT PATHOLOGIES GLOBALLY, THERE ARE NO DEFINITIVE TREATMENTS AVAILABLE. RECENTLY, RESEARCH HAS FOCUSED ON ELUCIDATING THE COMPLEX INTERPLAY THAT TAKES PLACE BETWEEN INFLAMMATORY PROCESSES AND EPIGENETIC REGULATION, SHOWING THAT HISTONE POST-TRANSLATIONAL MODIFICATIONS (PTMS) CAN EXERT A PRONOUNCED EFFECT ON THE EXPRESSION OF OA-RELATED GENES.
