3 3404 35 HOW EPIGENETICS IMPACTS ON HUMAN DISEASES. EPIGENETICS IS A RAPIDLY GROWING FIELD OF BIOLOGY THAT STUDIES THE CHANGES IN GENE EXPRESSION THAT ARE NOT DUE TO ALTERATIONS IN THE DNA SEQUENCE BUT RATHER THE CHEMICAL MODIFICATIONS OF DNA AND ITS ASSOCIATED PROTEINS. EPIGENETIC MECHANISMS CAN PROFOUNDLY INFLUENCE GENE EXPRESSION, CELL DIFFERENTIATION, TISSUE DEVELOPMENT, AND DISEASE SUSCEPTIBILITY. UNDERSTANDING EPIGENETIC CHANGES IS ESSENTIAL TO ELUCIDATE THE MECHANISMS UNDERLYING THE INCREASINGLY RECOGNIZED ROLE OF ENVIRONMENTAL AND LIFESTYLE FACTORS IN HEALTH AND DISEASE AND THE INTERGENERATIONAL TRANSMISSION OF PHENOTYPES. MOREOVER, EPIGENETICS PROVIDE INSIGHT INTO DISEASE PATHOGENESIS AND BIOMARKERS FOR DISEASE DIAGNOSIS AND RISK STRATIFICATION.
