4  4361  28 MIR-96 ACTS AS A TUMOR SUPPRESSOR VIA TARGETING THE BCR-ABL1 ONCOGENE IN CHRONIC MYELOID LEUKEMIA BLASTIC TRANSFORMATION. IN ADDITION, RE-EXPRESSION OF MIR-96 COULD INCREASE THE SENSITIVITY OF CML-BC CELLS TO IMATINIB. THUS, MIR-96 FUNCTIONS AS A TUMOR SUPPRESSOR, AND RE-EXPRESSION OF THIS MICRORNA MIGHT HAVE THERAPEUTIC BENEFITS IN CML BLASTIC TRANSFORMATION.	2019
