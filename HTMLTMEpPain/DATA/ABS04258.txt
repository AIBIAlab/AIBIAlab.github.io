17 5271  25 PROMOTER HYPERMETHYLATION OF PROGESTERONE RECEPTOR ISOFORM B (PR-B) IN ENDOMETRIOSIS. THE PHYSIOLOGICAL EFFECTS OF PROGESTERONE (P) ARE MEDIATED BY TWO ISOFORMS OF PROGESTERONE RECEPTORS (PRS): PR-A AND PR-B. PROGESTINS HAVE LONG BEEN USED IN THE TREATMENT OF ENDOMETRIOSIS BUT UNFORTUNATELY THE RELIEF OF PAIN IS RELATIVELY SHORT-TERM.
