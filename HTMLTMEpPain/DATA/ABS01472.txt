THE REGULATION OF THE 31 KDA PROIL-1BETA PRECURSOR CODED BY THE IL1B GENE HAS BEEN EXTENSIVELY STUDIED IN MYELOID CELLS, BUT NOT IN LYMPHOID-DERIVED CD4 T CELLS.
