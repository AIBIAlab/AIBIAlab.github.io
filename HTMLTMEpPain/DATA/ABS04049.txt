20 2925 39 GENERATING LONG-LIVED CD8(+) T-CELL MEMORY: INSIGHTS FROM EPIGENETIC PROGRAMS. IN ORDER TO EXPLOIT THIS POTENTIAL IN THE DESIGN OF NEW VACCINES, IT IS NECESSARY TO UNDERSTAND HOW AND WHEN MEMORY T CELLS ACQUIRE THEIR POISED EFFECTOR POTENTIAL, AND MOREOVER, HOW THEY MAINTAIN THESE PROPERTIES DURING HOMEOSTATIC PROLIFERATION.
