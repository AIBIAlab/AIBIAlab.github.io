FROM AN EVOLUTIONARY PERSPECTIVE, THE SELECTIVE PRESSURE EXERTED BY CHEMOTHERAPY LEADS TO THE EMERGENCE OF RESISTANT CLONES WHERE RESISTANCE CAN BE ASSOCIATED WITH MANY DIFFERENT FUNCTIONAL MECHANISMS AT THE SINGLE CELL LEVEL OR CAN INVOLVE CHANGES IN THE TUMOR MICRO-ENVIRONMENT.
