METHYLATION PROFILING ALLOWED US TO DETERMINE THAT ABERRANT METHYLATION IS COMMONLY PRESENT IN NORMAL AGING LIVERS, AND SEQUENTIALLY PROGRESSES WITH ADVANCING STAGES OF CHRONIC VIRAL INFECTION. FINALLY, OUR DATA PROVIDE EVIDENCE THAT HCV INFECTION MAY ACCELERATE THE METHYLATION PROCESS AND SUGGESTS A CONTINUUM OF INCREASING METHYLATION WITH PERSISTENT VIRAL INFECTION AND CARCINOGENESIS IN THE LIVER.	2008
