THE AUTHORS CARRIED OUT A FOCUSED REVIEW USING A SYSTEMS BIOLOGY APPROACH TO INTEGRATE THE COMPLEX MECHANISMS OF NOCICEPTION AND NEUROPATHIC PAIN, AND TO DECIPHER THE EFFECTS OF NITROUS OXIDE (N(2)O) ON THOSE PATHWAYS, BEYOND THE KNOWN EFFECT OF N(2)O ON N-METHYL-D-ASPARTATE RECEPTORS.
