6  3448  29 HYPERMETHYLATION OF THE N-MYC DOWNSTREAM-REGULATED GENE 2 PROMOTER IN PERIPHERAL BLOOD MONONUCLEAR CELLS IS ASSOCIATED WITH LIVER FIBROSIS IN CHRONIC HEPATITIS B. N-MYC DOWNSTREAM-REGULATED GENE (NDRG) 2 IS A CYTOPLASMIC PROTEIN AND PARTICIPATES IN THE PATHOGENESIS OF LIVER FIBROSIS. IN CONCLUSION, HYPERMETHYLATION OF NDRG2 IN PBMCS WAS CORRELATED WITH DECREASED MRNA EXPRESSION AND WITH LIVER FIBROSIS. THE METHYLATION STATUS OF THE NDRG2 PROMOTER IN PBMCS IS A POTENTIAL NONINVASIVE BIOMARKER TO PREDICT THE SEVERITY OF LIVER FIBROSIS.	2017

11  5459  35 RESEARCH ON THE EPIGENETIC REGULATION MECHANISM OF THE PTPN6 GENE IN ADVANCED CHRONIC MYELOID LEUKAEMIA.
