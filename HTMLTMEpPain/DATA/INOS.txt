1  1105 148 COMBINED INHIBITION OF HISTONE DEACETYLASES AND BET FAMILY PROTEINS AS EPIGENETIC THERAPY FOR NERVE INJURY-INDUCED NEUROPATHIC PAIN. CURRENT TREATMENTS FOR NEUROPATHIC PAIN HAVE OFTEN MODERATE EFFICACY AND PRESENT UNWANTED EFFECTS SHOWING THE NEED TO DEVELOP EFFECTIVE THERAPIES. ACCUMULATING EVIDENCE SUGGESTS THAT HISTONE ACETYLATION PLAYS ESSENTIAL ROLES IN CHRONIC PAIN AND THE ANALGESIC ACTIVITY OF HISTONE DEACETYLASES (HDACS) INHIBITORS IS DOCUMENTED. PRESENT RESULTS INDICATE A KEY ROLE OF ACETYLATED HISTONES AND THEIR RECRUITMENT BY BET PROTEINS ON MICROGLIA-MEDIATED SPINAL NEUROINFLAMMATION. BY IDENTIFYING A ROLE FOR THE INTERACTION BETWEEN BRD4 AND P65 IN CONTROLLING THE RESPONSE OF BETA-CELLS TO PROINFLAMMATORY CYTOKINES, WE PROVIDE MECHANISTIC INFORMATION ON HOW BET BROMODOMAIN INHIBITION CAN DECREASE INFLAMMATION.
