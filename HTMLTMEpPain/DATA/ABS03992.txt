ALSO, WE FOUND THAT 39 ECCDNA-TARGETING GENES WERE ASSOCIATED WITH ESRD, AND SOME OF THESE ECCDNAS MAY BE RELATED TO THE PATHOGENESIS OF ESRD. CONCLUSIONS: OUR FINDINGS REVEALED THE CHARACTERISTICS OF ECCDNA IN ESRD PATIENTS AND DISCOVERED POTENTIALLY HUB AND ESRD-RELEVANT ECCDNA-TARGETING GENES, SUGGESTING A NOVEL PROBABLE MECHANISM OF ESRD.	2023
