7  6910  16 [TRANSFORMING GROWTH FACTOR-BETA AND RENAL FIBROSIS]. 13  5992  27 TGF-BETA: THE MASTER REGULATOR OF FIBROSIS. TRANSFORMING GROWTH FACTOR-BETA (TGF-BETA) IS THE PRIMARY FACTOR THAT DRIVES FIBROSIS IN MOST, IF NOT ALL, FORMS OF CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE (CKD). INHIBITION OF THE TGF-BETA ISOFORM, TGF-BETA1, OR ITS DOWNSTREAM SIGNALLING PATHWAYS SUBSTANTIALLY LIMITS RENAL FIBROSIS IN A WIDE RANGE OF DISEASE MODELS WHEREAS OVEREXPRESSION OF TGF-BETA1 INDUCES RENAL FIBROSIS.
