17  4333  32 MICRORNAS: KEY PLAYERS IN MICROGLIA AND ASTROCYTE MEDIATED INFLAMMATION IN CNS PATHOLOGIES. THE SIGNIFICANCE OF MICROGLIA AND ASTROCYTES IN NEURAL DEVELOPMENT, IN MAINTAINING SYNAPTIC CONNECTIONS AND HOMEOSTASIS IN THE HEALTHY BRAIN IS WELL ESTABLISHED.
