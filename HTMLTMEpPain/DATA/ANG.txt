INFLAMMATION, ABERRANT METHYLATION, AND DYSFUNCTION WERE OBSERVED IN THE HYPERTENSIVE KIDNEY, AND THESE EFFECTS WERE ALLEVIATED WITH H(2)S SUPPLEMENTATION. WE IDENTIFY MIR-129 AS A POTENTIAL REGULATOR OF BLOOD PRESSURE AND H(2)S REGULATION.	2017
