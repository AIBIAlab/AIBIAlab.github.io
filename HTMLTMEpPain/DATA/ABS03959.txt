AUTOIMMUNE CONNECTIVE TISSUE DISEASES (ACTDS) ENCOMPASS A HETEROGENEOUS GROUP OF CHRONIC IMMUNE-MEDIATED INFLAMMATORY DISORDERS, PRIMARILY AFFECTING CONNECTIVE TISSUES AND CLINICALLY CHARACTERIZED BY VARIABLE MULTISYSTEM MANIFESTATIONS, FREQUENTLY OVERLAPPING.
