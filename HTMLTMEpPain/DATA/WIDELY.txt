17  1041  34 CLINICAL AND MOLECULAR ASPECTS OF LEAD TOXICITY: AN UPDATE. LEAD TOXICITY IS A MAJOR PUBLIC HEALTH ISSUE IN DEVELOPED AND DEVELOPING COUNTRIES. THE MOLECULAR BASIS OF LEAD EXPOSURE RESULTING IN VARIOUS SYSTEMIC EFFECTS IS BEING EXTENSIVELY EXPLORED.
