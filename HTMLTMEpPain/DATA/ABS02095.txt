THERE ARE THREE CONNECTED MOLECULAR MECHANISMS OF EPIGENETIC CELLULAR MEMORY IN MAMMALIAN CELLS: DNA METHYLATION, HISTONE MODIFICATIONS, AND RNA INTERFERENCE. THE FIRST TWO HAVE NOW BEEN FIRMLY LINKED TO NEOPLASTIC TRANSFORMATION. EPIGENETIC MODIFICATIONS, INVOLVING DNA METHYLATION AND HISTONE MODIFICATIONS, ARE MAINTAINED UPON SOMATIC CELL REPLICATION, AND ARE FUNDAMENTAL MECHANISMS FOR CELLULAR MEMORY. DNA METHYLATION OF PROMOTER CPG ISLANDS OF TUMOR-SUPPRESSOR GENES CAN SILENCE THEIR DOWNSTREAM GENES, AND CAN BE CAUSALLY INVOLVED IN CANCER DEVELOPMENT AND PROGRESSION.
