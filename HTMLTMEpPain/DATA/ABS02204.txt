CONCLUSIONS: TGF-BETA UP-REGULATES IGFBP-3 THROUGH GIPC, LEADING TO INCREASED HSC MIGRATION IN VITRO AND PROMOTES PORTAL HYPERTENSION IN VIVO. 11 3096  37 GENOMIC CHARACTERIZATION REVEALS NOVEL MECHANISMS UNDERLYING THE VALOSIN-CONTAINING PROTEIN-MEDIATED CARDIAC PROTECTION AGAINST HEART FAILURE. OUR PREVIOUS STUDIES FOUND THAT THE VALOSIN-CONTAINING PROTEIN (VCP), AN ATPASE-ASSOCIATED PROTEIN, WAS SIGNIFICANTLY DECREASED IN THE HYPERTENSIVE HEART TISSUES.
