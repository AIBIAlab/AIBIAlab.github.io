DETECTION OF PROMOTER HYPERMETHYLATION OF TUMOR SUPPRESSOR GENES IN EXFOLIATED CELLS FROM THE LUNG PROVIDES AN ASSESSMENT OF FIELD CANCERIZATION THAT IN TURN PREDICTS LUNG CANCER. THE ASSOCIATIONS COULD BE MEDIATED BY GABAERGIC SIGNALING THAT DRIVES THE SMOKING-INDUCED MUCOUS CELL METAPLASIA. THIS PATTERN OF EXPRESSION HAS MADE THEM PROMISING TARGETS FOR IMMUNOTHERAPY AND CANCER DETECTION. OUR AIM WAS TO FIND NEW MEMBERS OF THIS GROUP THAT MIGHT BE USEFUL AS MARKERS IN THE DETECTION OF CANCER AND IMMUNOTHERAPY. DESIGN AND SETTING: A DESCRIPTIVE STUDY CONDUCTED IN REFERRAL CENTERS OF TEHRAN UNIVERSITY OF MEDICAL SCIENCE FROM JANUARY 2008 TO JANUARY 2009. RATIONALE: SYSTEMIC GLUCOCORTICOIDS ARE USED THERAPEUTICALLY TO TREAT A VARIETY OF MEDICAL CONDITIONS.
