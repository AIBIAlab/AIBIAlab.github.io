THESE DATA OPEN UP THE POSSIBILITY THAT EPIGENETIC MODULATORS MIGHT BE GIVEN IN COMBINATION WITH "TRADITIONAL" DRUGS IN A CONTEXT OF A MULTI TARGET APPROACH FOR A BETTER ANALGESIC EFFICACY.	2014
