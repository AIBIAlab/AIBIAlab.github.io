1  135 130 ABERRANT CPG METHYLATION MEDIATES ABNORMAL TRANSCRIPTION OF MAO-A INDUCED BY ACUTE AND CHRONIC L-3,4-DIHYDROXYPHENYLALANINE ADMINISTRATION IN SH-SY5Y NEURONAL CELLS. MONOAMINE OXIDASE A (MAO-A) IS ONE OF THE KEY ENZYMES IN DOPAMINE METABOLISM AND THEREFORE MAY BE INVOLVED IN L-DOPA-INDUCED SIDE EFFECTS. AND, EPIGENETIC MODIFICATION CONTROLS MAO-A GENE TRANSCRIPTION. TRANSCRIPTION OF TEN-ELEVEN TRANSLOCATION 1 (TET1) WERE SIGNIFICANTLY DOWNREGULATED IN CHRONIC L-DOPA ADMINISTRATION. THESE DATA INDICATED THAT IN CHRONIC L-DOPA ADMINISTRATION, TET1 DOWNREGULATION MIGHT MEDIATE CPG HYPERMETHYLATION, WHICH IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE DOWNREGULATION OF MAO-A TRANSCRIPTION. IN CONTRAST, IN ACUTE L-DOPA ADMINISTRATION, DNMT3A DOWNREGULATION MIGHT MEDIATE HYPOMETHYLATION, CONTRIBUTING TO THE MAO-A UPREGULATION. IN CONCLUSION, OUR FINDINGS SUGGESTED THAT TET1 AND DNMTS MIGHT MEDIATE ABERRANT CPG METHYLATION, ASSOCIATED WITH THE MISREGULATION OF MAO-A IN L-DOPA ADMINISTRATION, WHICH MIGHT CONTRIBUTE TO DOPAMINE RELEASE ABNORMALLY LEADING TO THE SIDE EFFECTS OF L-DOPA.	2017
