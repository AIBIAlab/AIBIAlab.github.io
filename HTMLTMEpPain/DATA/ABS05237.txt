CONCLUSIONS: IN THIS STUDY, FIFTEEN MICRORNAS WHICH WERE MODULATED IN HEPATIC TISSUES AND IN TUMORS DURING THE TRANSITION NAFLD-NASH-HCC ARE REPORTED. BESIDES SOME ALREADY DESCRIBED, NEW AND EARLY DYSREGULATED MIRNAS WERE IDENTIFIED. FUNCTIONAL ANALYSES ARE NEEDED TO VALIDATE THE RESULTS HERE OBTAINED, AND TO BETTER DEFINE THE ROLE OF THESE MOLECULES IN THE PROGRESSION OF THE HEPATIC DISEASE.	2016
