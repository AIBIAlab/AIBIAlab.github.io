5  4931  64 PATERNAL ALCOHOL EXPOSURE REDUCES ALCOHOL DRINKING AND INCREASES BEHAVIORAL SENSITIVITY TO ALCOHOL SELECTIVELY IN MALE OFFSPRING. 12  6639  35 UNRAVELING THE EPIGENOMIC AND TRANSCRIPTOMIC INTERPLAY DURING ALCOHOL-INDUCED ANXIOLYSIS.
