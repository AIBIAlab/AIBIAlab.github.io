ADDITIONALLY, IT HAS BEEN DEMONSTRATED THAT TRAININGS GREATLY MINIMIZE EXPOSURE-CAUSING BEHAVIOURS. CONCLUSION: IT WAS DISCOVERED THAT WHILE THE DURATION OF A SINGLE TRAINING DOES NOT MAKE A MEANINGFUL EFFECT, THE CONTINUING OF REMINDER TRAININGS DID MAKE A SUBSTANTIAL DIFFERENCE IN THE URINE BPA LEVEL.	2022
