6  2122  33 EPIGENETIC IMPACT OF INFECTION ON CARCINOGENESIS: MECHANISMS AND APPLICATIONS. VIRAL AND BACTERIAL INFECTIONS ARE INVOLVED IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF HUMAN CANCERS, SUCH AS LIVER, NASOPHARYNGEAL, CERVICAL, HEAD AND NECK, AND GASTRIC CANCERS. FURTHERMORE, SUPPRESSION OF ABERRANT DNA METHYLATION WAS SHOWN TO BE A USEFUL STRATEGY FOR CANCER PREVENTION IN AN ANIMAL MODEL. HERE, WE REVIEW THE INVOLVEMENT OF ABERRANT DNA METHYLATION IN VARIOUS TYPES OF INFECTION-ASSOCIATED CANCERS, ALONG WITH INDIVIDUAL INDUCTION MECHANISMS, AND WE DISCUSS THE APPLICATION OF THESE FINDINGS FOR CANCER PREVENTION, DIAGNOSIS, AND THERAPY.	2016
