19 3638  36 INCREASED EXPRESSION OF BETA-DEFENSIN 1 (DEFB1) IN CHRONIC OBSTRUCTIVE PULMONARY DISEASE. ON-GOING AIRWAY INFLAMMATION IS CHARACTERISTIC FOR THE PATHOPHYSIOLOGY OF CHRONIC OBSTRUCTIVE PULMONARY DISEASE (COPD). WE CAN DEMONSTRATE THAT DEFB1 MRNA EXPRESSION WAS SIGNIFICANTLY INCREASED IN BRONCHOPULMONARY SPECIMEN OF PATIENTS WITH COPD (N = 34) VS. HEALTHY CONTROLS (N = 10) (P<0.0001). THUS, EXPRESSION OF HUMAN DEFB1 IS UPREGULATED AND RELATED TO THE DECREASE IN PULMONARY FUNCTION IN PATIENTS WITH COPD.	2011
