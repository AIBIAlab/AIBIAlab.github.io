ONSET AND DEVELOPMENT OF METASTASIS REPRESENTS A DETRIMENTAL EVENT THAT WORSEN THE PATIENT'S PROGNOSIS BY PROFOUNDLY INFLUENCING THE QUALITY OF LIFE AND IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE OMINOUS PROGRESSION OF THE DISEASE.
