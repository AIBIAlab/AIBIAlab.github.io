20 3224  33 HELICOBACTER PYLORI INFECTION AND STEM CELLS AT THE ORIGIN OF GASTRIC CANCER. CASE-CONTROL STUDIES HAVE SHOWN AN ASSOCIATION BETWEEN THE TWO, RECOGNIZED AS EARLY AS 1994 AND FURTHER SUBSTANTIATED BY INTERVENTIONAL STUDIES IN WHICH H.
