THE DECREASE IN H3K27ME2/3 AND INCREASE IN JMJD3 RECRUITMENT WERE CONFIRMED BY IN VIVO STUDIES USING A SCHISTOSOMA MANSONI EGG-CHALLENGED MOUSE MODEL, A WELL-STUDIED SYSTEM KNOWN TO SUPPORT AN M2 PHENOTYPE. COLLECTIVELY, THESE DATA INDICATE THAT CHROMATIN REMODELING IS MECHANISTICALLY IMPORTANT IN THE ACQUISITION OF THE M2-MACROPHAGE PHENOTYPE.	2009
