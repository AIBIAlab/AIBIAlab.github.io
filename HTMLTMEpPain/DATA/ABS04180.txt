1 4180 150 MERCURY EXPOSURE INDUCES CYTOSKELETON DISRUPTION AND LOSS OF RENAL FUNCTION THROUGH EPIGENETIC MODULATION OF MMP9 EXPRESSION. MERCURY IS ONE OF THE MAJOR HEAVY METAL POLLUTANTS OCCURRING IN ELEMENTAL, INORGANIC AND ORGANIC FORMS. ANIMAL AND EPIDEMIOLOGICAL STUDIES INDICATE THAT MEHG AFFECTS NEURAL AND RENAL FUNCTION. OUR STUDY IS FOCUSED ON NEPHROTOXIC POTENTIAL OF MEHG. 2 3715  39 INHERITED EFFECTS OF LOW-DOSE EXPOSURE TO METHYLMERCURY IN NEURAL STEM CELLS. METHYLMERCURY (MEHG) IS AN ENVIRONMENTAL CONTAMINANT WITH RECOGNIZED NEUROTOXIC EFFECTS, PARTICULARLY TO THE DEVELOPING NERVOUS SYSTEM. IN THE PRESENT STUDY, WE SHOW THAT NANOMOLAR CONCENTRATIONS OF MEHG CAN INDUCE LONG-LASTING EFFECTS IN NEURAL STEM CELLS (NSCS).
