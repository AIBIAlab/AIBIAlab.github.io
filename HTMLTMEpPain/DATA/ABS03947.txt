THE EXPRESSION IN COLON WAS SIGNIFICANTLY HIGHER COMPARED TO ANY GASTRIC TISSUES SUGGESTING FUNCTIONAL DIFFERENCE. IN SUMMARY, MIR-137 METHYLATION IS A FREQUENT EVENT IN GASTROINTESTINAL CANCERS WHICH OCCURS EARLY IN STEPWISE MANNER DURING GASTRIC CARCINOGENESIS AND INVERSELY CORRELATES WITH GLOBAL METHYLATION.
