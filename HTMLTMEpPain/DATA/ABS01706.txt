1 1706 130 DYNORPHIN/KOP AND NOCICEPTIN/NOP GENE EXPRESSION AND EPIGENETIC CHANGES BY COCAINE IN RAT STRIATUM AND NUCLEUS ACCUMBENS. COCAINE INDUCES NEUROCHEMICAL CHANGES OF ENDOGENOUS PRODYNORPHIN-KAPPA OPIOID RECEPTOR (PDYN-KOP) AND PRONOCICEPTIN/ORPHANINFQ-NOCICEPTIN RECEPTOR (PN/OFQ-NOP) SYSTEMS.
