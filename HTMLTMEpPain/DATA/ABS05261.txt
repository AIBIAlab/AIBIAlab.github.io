1 5261 129 PROGRESSIVE SILENCING OF THE ZINC TRANSPORTER ZIP8 (SLC39A8) IN CHRONIC CADMIUM-EXPOSED LUNG EPITHELIAL CELLS. CADMIUM (CD), A NON-ESSENTIAL METAL, STEALTHILY ENTERS THE CELLS BY UTILIZING THE ESSENTIAL METAL IMPORTING PATHWAYS. THE ZINC TRANSPORTERS ZIP8, ZIP14, AND DIVALENT METAL TRANSPORTER 1 (DMT1) ARE NOW EMERGING AS SEVERAL IMPORTANT METAL TRANSPORTERS INVOLVED IN CELLULAR CD INCORPORATION AND THEIR EXPRESSIONS HAVE BEEN SHOWN TO BE DOWN-REGULATED IN SEVERAL CD-RESISTANT (CDR) CELL LINES, HOWEVER, THE INVOLVEMENT OF THESE TRANSPORTERS DURING THE DEVELOPMENT OF CD-RESISTANCE IN LUNG CELLS IS UNCLEAR. UNDERSTANDING THE PATHOLOGICAL PROCESSES AT PLAY WILL DIRECT THE APPLICATION OF NOVEL THERAPEUTIC AVENUES. HYPOXIA HAS BEEN IMPLICATED IN THE PATHOGENESIS OF PULMONARY FIBROSIS YET THE PRECISE MECHANISM BY WHICH IT CONTRIBUTES TO DISEASE PROGRESSION REMAINS TO BE FULLY ELUCIDATED. MSPCR REVEALED THAT THY-1 BECAME METHYLATED FOLLOWING FIBROBLAST EXPOSURE TO 1% O2. CONCLUSION: THESE DATA SUGGEST THAT GLOBAL AND GENE-SPECIFIC CHANGES IN DNA METHYLATION MAY PLAY AN IMPORTANT ROLE IN FIBROBLAST FUNCTION IN HYPOXIA.	2012
