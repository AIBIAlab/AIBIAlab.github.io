13 4335  42 MICRORNAS: SMALL MOLECULES WITH SIGNIFICANT FUNCTIONS, PARTICULARLY IN THE CONTEXT OF VIRAL HEPATITIS B AND C INFECTION. A MICRORNA (MIRNA) IS DEFINED AS A SMALL MOLECULE OF NON-CODING RNA (NCRNA). ITS MOLECULAR SIZE IS ABOUT 20 NUCLEOTIDES (NT), AND IT ACTS ON GENE EXPRESSION'S REGULATION AT THE POST-TRANSCRIPTION LEVEL THROUGH BINDING TO THE 3'UNTRANSLATED REGIONS (UTR), CODING SEQUENCES, OR 5'UTR OF THE TARGET MESSENGER RNAS (MRNAS), WHICH LEADS TO THE SUPPRESSION OR DEGRADATION OF THE MRNA. 16  827  37 CHARACTERIZATION OF LONG NON-CODING RNAS IN SYSTEMIC SCLEROSIS MONOCYTES: A POTENTIAL ROLE FOR PSMB8-AS1 IN ALTERED CYTOKINE SECRETION. THE EPIGENETIC REVOLUTION HAS ARRIVED FOR BIOLOGY. THE SIGNIFICANT PROGRESS IN EPIGENETIC STUDIES HAVE ALLOWED US, TO UNDERSTAND NEW LOOKS IN THE PHYSIOLOGY AND PATHOPHYSIOLOGY OF EMBRYONIC DEVELOPMENT, CANCER AND OTHER CHRONIC DISEASES. SPECIFIC MOLECULAR EPIGENETIC ALTERATIONS IN DIFFERENT CANCER TYPES, GIVE US NEW STRATEGIES TO DESIGN IMPROVED CANCER THERAPY. 18 6906  27 [THE ROLE OF GLYCANS IN CANCER DEVELOPMENT AND PROGRESSION. CLINICAL APPLICATIONS].
