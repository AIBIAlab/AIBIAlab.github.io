HYPERKATIFEIA PROVIDES AN ADDITIONAL SOURCE OF MOTIVATION FOR COMPULSIVE DRUG SEEKING VIA NEGATIVE REINFORCEMENT. NEGATIVE REINFORCEMENT REFLECTS AN INCREASE IN THE PROBABILITY OF A RESPONSE TO REMOVE AN AVERSIVE STIMULUS OR DRUG SEEKING TO REMOVE HYPERKATIFEIA THAT IS AUGMENTED BY GENETIC/EPIGENETIC VULNERABILITY, ENVIRONMENTAL TRAUMA, AND PSYCHIATRIC COMORBIDITY. FROM NEUROSCIENCE, WE'VE SEEN THAT STRESS ACTIVATION IMPACTS BEHAVIOR, ENDOCRINE AND SOMATIC PHYSIOLOGY AND INFLUENCES NEUROCHEMICAL EVENTS THAT ONE CAN CAPTURE IN REAL TIME WITH CURRENT IMAGING TECHNOLOGIES.
