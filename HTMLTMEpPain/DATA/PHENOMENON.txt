LONG-TERM ADAPTIVE CHANGES OCCURRING IN A DEVELOPING FETUS IN RESPONSE TO UNSTABLE IN UTERO ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS, WHICH APPEAR AT A PARTICULAR TIME (CRITICAL WINDOW), ARE CALLED INTRAUTERINE OR FETAL PROGRAMMING.
