1 3083 152 GENOME-WIDE SCREEN OF DNA METHYLATION CHANGES INDUCED BY LOW DOSE X-RAY RADIATION IN MICE. EPIGENETIC MECHANISMS PLAY A KEY ROLE IN NON-TARGETED EFFECTS OF RADIATION. THIRTY MALE BALB/C MICE WERE DIVIDED INTO 3 GROUPS: CONTROL, ACUTELY EXPOSED (0.5 GY X-RAYS), AND CHRONIC EXPOSURE FOR 10 DAYS (0.05GY/DX10D). 9 2262  28 EPIGENETIC PROFILING IN CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA REVEALS NOVEL METHYLATION TARGETS. CPG ISLAND METHYLATION IS AN EPIGENETIC ALTERATION THAT CONTRIBUTES TO TUMORIGENESIS BY TRANSCRIPTIONAL INACTIVATION OF GENES.
