2 3532  44 IMATINIB INDEPENDENT ABERRANT METHYLATION OF NOV/CCN3 IN CHRONIC MYELOGENOUS LEUKEMIA PATIENTS: A MECHANISM UPSTREAM OF BCR-ABL1 FUNCTION? 11 1272  40 CYTOTOXIC ACTIVITY OF VALPROIC ACID ON PRIMARY CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA CELLS. BACKGROUND: CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA (CLL) IS THE MOST COMMON ADULT LEUKEMIA IN WESTERN CIVILIZATION. THE ACCUMULATION OF CD5+CD19+ B LYMPHOCYTES IN PERIPHERAL BLOOD IS DUE TO A DEFECT IN THE APOPTOTIC PATHWAY RATHER THAN EXCESSIVE PROLIFERATION IN THE BONE MARROW AND LYMPH NODES. CONCLUSIONS: THE RESULTS OF THE PRESENT EXPERIMENTS FOUND THAT VPA AT A CLINICALLY APPLICABLE CONCENTRATION SIGNIFICANTLY INDUCES APOPTOSIS INDEPENDENTLY OF THE DISEASE STAGE AND MIGHT BE A VALUABLE THERAPEUTIC AGENT FOR ALL CLL PATIENTS.	2015

INTERESTINGLY, HIF1A WAS NOT ALTERED WITH CHRONIC HYPOXIA IN ANY OF THE STUDIES. FURTHERMORE, WE COMPARED OUR DATA TO THREE OTHER STUDIES THAT IDENTIFIED HIF-RESPONSIVE GENES BY VARIOUS APPROACHES.
