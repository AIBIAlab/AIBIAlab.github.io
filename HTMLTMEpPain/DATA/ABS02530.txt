15  528  31 ASTHMA EPIGENETICS. EPIGENETICS IS THE STUDY OF MITOTICALLY OR MEIOTICALLY HERITABLE CHANGES IN GENE EXPRESSION THAT OCCUR WITHOUT DIRECTLY ALTERING THE DNA SEQUENCE. MICRORNAS ARE SHORT ~22 NUCLEOTIDE LONG, NON-CODING, SINGLE-STRANDED RNAS THAT BINDS TO COMPLEMENTARY SEQUENCES IN THE TARGET MRNAS, USUALLY RESULTING IN GENE SILENCING.
