ANGIOTENSIN-CONVERTING ENZYME (ACE) APPEARS AS ONE CANDIDATE GENE THAT BRINGS NEW INSIGHTS INTO THE EPIGENETIC CONTROL AND LATER DEVELOPMENT OF DISEASES.
