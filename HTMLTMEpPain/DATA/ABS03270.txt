13 1348 28 DETERMINANTS OF FIBROSIS PROGRESSION AND REGRESSION IN NASH. CIRRHOSIS HAS BECOME THE MAJOR LIVER-RELATED CLINICAL ENDPOINT IN NON-ALCOHOLIC STEATOHEPATITIS (NASH). HOWEVER, PROGRESSION TO CIRRHOSIS IS LESS PREDICTABLE IN NASH THAN IN OTHER CHRONIC LIVER DISEASES. THIS IS DUE TO THE COMPLEX AND MULTIFACTORIAL AETIOLOGY OF NASH, WHICH IS DETERMINED BY LIFESTYLE AND NUTRITION, MULTIPLE GENETIC AND EPIGENETIC FACTORS, AND A PROMINENT ROLE OF HEPATIC AND EXTRAHEPATIC COMORBIDITIES.
