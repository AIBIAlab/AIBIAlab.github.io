THE MODEL PERFORMANCE WAS INDEPENDENT OF CANCER STAGE. THIS STUDY BRINGS US CLOSER TO A CLINICAL USEFUL DIAGNOSTIC MARKER FOR PANCREATIC CANCER, WHICH IS URGENTLY NEEDED. IN VIEW OF THE ADVERSE EFFECTS OF 5-AZA-DC, DNMT-TARGETED STRATEGY HAS BEEN PROPOSED AND MAY PROVE TO BE MORE EFFECTIVE THAN DEMETHYLATING AGENTS.	2012
