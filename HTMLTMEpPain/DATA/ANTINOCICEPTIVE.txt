2  2407 39 EPIGENETIC RESTORATION OF VOLTAGE-GATED POTASSIUM CHANNEL KV1.2 ALLEVIATES NERVE INJURY-INDUCED NEUROPATHIC PAIN. 11  3201 45 HDAC2 IN PRIMARY SENSORY NEURONS CONSTITUTIVELY RESTRAINS CHRONIC PAIN BY REPRESSING ALPHA2DELTA-1 EXPRESSION AND ASSOCIATED NMDA RECEPTOR ACTIVITY.
