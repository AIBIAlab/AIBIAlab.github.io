FIBROMYALGIA (FM), CHARACTERIZED BY CHRONIC WIDESPREAD PAIN, FATIGUE, AND COGNITIVE/MOOD DISTURBANCES, LEADS TO REDUCED WORKPLACE PRODUCTIVITY AND INCREASED HEALTHCARE EXPENSES. TO DETERMINE IF ACQUIRED EPIGENETIC/GENETIC CHANGES ARE ASSOCIATED WITH FM, WE COMPARED THE FREQUENCY OF SPONTANEOUSLY OCCURRING MICRONUCLEI (MN) AND GENOME-WIDE METHYLATION PATTERNS IN WOMEN WITH FM (N = 10) TO THOSE SEEN IN COMPARABLY AGED HEALTHY CONTROLS (N = 42 (MN); N = 8 (METHYLATION)).
