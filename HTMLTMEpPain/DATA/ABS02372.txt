HERE, WE SHOW THAT THE BROMODOMAIN PROTEIN TGBDP1 IS ESSENTIAL FOR PARASITE SURVIVAL AND THAT LOSS OF TGBDP1 RESULTS IN GLOBAL DYSREGULATION OF GENE EXPRESSION.
