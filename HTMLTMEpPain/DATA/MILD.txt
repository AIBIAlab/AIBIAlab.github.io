INTERESTINGLY, AGOMELATINE COULD NORMALIZE BOTH THE BEHAVIORAL AND THE MOLECULAR ALTERATIONS INDUCED BY STRESS, PROVIDING FURTHER INSIGHTS INTO THE MECHANISM OF ACTION OF THIS NEW GENERATION ANTIDEPRESSANT DRUG.	2014
