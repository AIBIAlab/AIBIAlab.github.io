19  790  30 CELLULAR AND MOLECULAR MECHANISMS OF CD8(+) T CELL DIFFERENTIATION, DYSFUNCTION AND EXHAUSTION. 20 2446  23 EPIGENETIC STRATEGIES SYNERGIZE WITH PD-L1/PD-1 TARGETED CANCER IMMUNOTHERAPIES TO ENHANCE ANTITUMOR RESPONSES. IMMUNOTHERAPY STRATEGIES TARGETING THE PROGRAMMED CELL DEATH LIGAND 1 (PD-L1)/PROGRAMMED CELL DEATH 1 (PD-1) PATHWAY IN CLINICAL TREATMENTS HAVE ACHIEVED REMARKABLE SUCCESS IN TREATING MULTIPLE TYPES OF CANCER. HOWEVER, OWING TO THE HETEROGENEITY OF TUMORS AND INDIVIDUAL IMMUNE SYSTEMS, PD-L1/PD-1 BLOCKADE STILL SHOWS SLOW RESPONSE RATES IN CONTROLLING MALIGNANCIES IN MANY PATIENTS.
