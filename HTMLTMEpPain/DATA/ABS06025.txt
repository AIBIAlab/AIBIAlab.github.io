15 2651  36 EPIGENOMICS AND TRANSCRIPTOMICS IN THE PREDICTION AND DIAGNOSIS OF CHILDHOOD ASTHMA: ARE WE THERE YET? ASTHMA IS THE MOST COMMON NON-COMMUNICABLE CHRONIC DISEASE OF CHILDHOOD. EPIGENOMIC STUDIES OFFER SIGNIFICANT ADVANTAGES OVER PREVIOUS APPROACHES. EPIGENETIC REGULATION IS HIGHLY TISSUE-SPECIFIC, AND CAN INDUCE BOTH SHORT- AND LONG-TERM CHANGES IN GENE EXPRESSION.
