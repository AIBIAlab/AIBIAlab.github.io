19 6067  41 THE DIABETES MELLITUS-ATHEROSCLEROSIS CONNECTION: THE ROLE OF LIPID AND GLUCOSE METABOLISM AND CHRONIC INFLAMMATION. DIABETES MELLITUS COMPRISES A GROUP OF CARBOHYDRATE METABOLISM DISORDERS THAT SHARE A COMMON MAIN FEATURE OF CHRONIC HYPERGLYCEMIA THAT RESULTS FROM DEFECTS OF INSULIN SECRETION, INSULIN ACTION, OR BOTH.
