LINE-1, A MARKER OF GLOBAL METHYLATION, INDICATED NO SIGNIFICANT DIFFERENCES BETWEEN THE THREE GROUPS OR THE TIME POINTS, ALTHOUGH METHYLATION OF TYPE 2 DIABETICS TENDED TO INCREASE OVER TIME.
