MECHANICALLY, THE DEPLETION OF EZH2 OR EZH2 INHIBITOR GSK343 WAS ABLE TO ENHANCE THE EXPRESSION AND THE PHOSPHORYLATION OF JANUS KINASE 2 (JK2) AND SIGNAL TRANSDUCER AND ACTIVATOR OF TRANSCRIPTION IN THE NCM460 AND FHC CELLS. CONCLUSION: THUS, WE CONCLUDED THAT EZH2 CONTRIBUTED TO APOPTOSIS AND INFLAMMATORY RESPONSE BY INACTIVATING JAK2/ SIGNAL TRANSDUCER AND ACTIVATOR OF TRANSCRIPTION SIGNALING IN IBD.
