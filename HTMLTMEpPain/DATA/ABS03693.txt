CERTAIN BACTERIA INCLUDING HELICOBACTER PYLORI DIRECTLY INTERACT WITH HOST CELLS, INDUCE PROINFLAMMATORY CYTOKINES AND STIMULATE PRODUCTION OF FREE RADICALS.
