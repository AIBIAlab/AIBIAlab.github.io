DIABETES IS THE LEADING CAUSE OF CKD. THEIR COMMON PATHOLOGICAL MANIFESTATION IS RENAL FIBROSIS. APPROXIMATELY HALF OF ALL PATIENTS WITH TYPE 2 DIABETES AND ONE-THIRD WITH TYPE 1 DIABETES WILL DEVELOP CKD. HOWEVER, RENAL FIBROSIS MECHANISMS ARE STILL POORLY UNDERSTOOD, ESPECIALLY POST-TRANSCRIPTIONAL AND EPIGENETIC REGULATION.
