DUCHENNE MUSCULAR DYSTROPHY (DMD) IS CHARACTERIZED BY CHRONIC SKELETAL MUSCLE NECROSIS, LEADING TO MUSCLE REGENERATION FAILURE AND FIBROSIS. ALTHOUGH MACROPHAGES (MPS) ARE NORMALLY ESSENTIAL FOR MUSCLE REGENERATION, DYSREGULATED MP FUNCTION PROMOTES PATHOLOGICAL MUSCLE REMODELLING.
