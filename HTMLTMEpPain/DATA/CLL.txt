20    27  26 A B-CELL EPIGENETIC SIGNATURE DEFINES THREE BIOLOGIC SUBGROUPS OF CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA WITH CLINICAL IMPACT. PROSPECTIVE IDENTIFICATION OF PATIENTS WITH CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA (CLL) DESTINED TO PROGRESS WOULD GREATLY FACILITATE THEIR CLINICAL MANAGEMENT. RECENTLY, WHOLE-GENOME DNA METHYLATION ANALYSES IDENTIFIED THREE CLINICOBIOLOGIC CLL SUBGROUPS WITH AN EPIGENETIC SIGNATURE RELATED TO DIFFERENT NORMAL B-CELL COUNTERPARTS.
