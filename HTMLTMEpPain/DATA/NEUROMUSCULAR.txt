IN ADDITION, THE SECRETED ADIPOKINE PATTERN CHANGES FROM ANTI-INFLAMMATORY TO PROINFLAMMATORY. DIACYLGLYCEROL (DAG) ACTIVATES SPECIFIC ISOFORMS OF PROTEIN KINASE C (EPSILON IN THE LIVER AND TAU IN THE MUSCULATURE), WHICH IN TURN LEAD TO INHIBITION OF THE INSULIN SIGNALING PATHWAY.
