IN OUR STUDY, WE USED AN IN VITRO MODEL OF HUMAN PRIMARY MUSCLE CELL CULTURES, DERIVED FROM IIM PATIENTS BEFORE AND AFTER A 6-MONTH INTENSIVE SUPERVISED TRAINING INTERVENTION TO ASSESS THE IMPACT OF DISEASE AND EXERCISE ON LIPID METABOLISM DYNAMICS. ABSTRACT: EXERCISE IMPROVES SKELETAL MUSCLE FUNCTION, CLINICAL STATE AND QUALITY OF LIFE IN PATIENTS WITH IDIOPATHIC INFLAMMATORY MYOPATHY (IIM). VASTUS LATERALIS; BERGSTROM NEEDLE BIOPSY) BEFORE AND AFTER TRAINING FROM PATIENTS AND SEDENTARY AGE/SEX/BODY MASS INDEX-MATCHED CONTROLS. OUR OBSERVATIONS SUGGEST THAT METABOLIC DEFECTS INTRINSIC TO SKELETAL MUSCLE COULD REPRESENT NON-IMMUNE PATHOMECHANISMS, WHICH CAN CONTRIBUTE TO MUSCLE WEAKNESS IN IIM.
