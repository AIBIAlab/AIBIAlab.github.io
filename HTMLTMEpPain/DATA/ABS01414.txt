EPIGENETIC MECHANISMS CAN PROFOUNDLY INFLUENCE GENE EXPRESSION, CELL DIFFERENTIATION, TISSUE DEVELOPMENT, AND DISEASE SUSCEPTIBILITY. UNDERSTANDING EPIGENETIC CHANGES IS ESSENTIAL TO ELUCIDATE THE MECHANISMS UNDERLYING THE INCREASINGLY RECOGNIZED ROLE OF ENVIRONMENTAL AND LIFESTYLE FACTORS IN HEALTH AND DISEASE AND THE INTERGENERATIONAL TRANSMISSION OF PHENOTYPES.
