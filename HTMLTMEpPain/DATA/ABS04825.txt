THE AIM OF THIS STUDY WAS TO INVESTIGATE THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN METHYLATION PROFILE IN THE MTHFR GENE PROMOTER AND BIOCHEMICAL, INFLAMMATORY AND OXIDATIVE STRESS MARKERS IN INDIVIDUALS WITH TYPE 2 DIABETES (T2DM) WHO HAVE BEEN DIAGNOSED FOR 5-10 YEARS WITH OR WITHOUT DIABETIC RETINOPATHY (DR) AND NEPHROPATHY (DN). CONCLUSIONS: HYPERMETHYLATION IN THE PROMOTER OF THE MTHFR GENE IS ASSOCIATED WITH THE OCCURRENCE OF DR AND WITH BIOCHEMICAL, INFLAMMATORY AND OXIDATIVE STRESS PARAMETERS IN THE CONTEXT OF CHRONIC COMPLICATIONS.	2017
