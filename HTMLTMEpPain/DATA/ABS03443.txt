CONCLUSIONS: DURING ALCOHOL WITHDRAWAL, THE CEA EXPERIENCES RAPID CHANGES IN MRNA EXPRESSION OF THESE FUNCTIONALLY RELATED TRANSCRIPTS THAT WERE NOT PREDICTED BY MEASUREMENT DURING CHRONIC EXPOSURE. THIS STUDY PROVIDES NEW INSIGHT INTO DYNAMIC EXPRESSION CHANGES DURING ALCOHOL WITHDRAWAL AND SUGGESTS NOVEL REGULATORY RELATIONSHIPS THAT POTENTIALLY IMPACT THE ASPECTS OF EMOTIONAL MODULATION.	2013
