RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS (RA) IS A CHRONIC SYSTEMIC AUTOIMMUNE DISEASE IN WHICH A PLETHORA OF EPIGENETIC CHANGES TAKE PLACE. CURRENT RESEARCH ON RA EPIGENETICS HAS FOCUSED MAINLY ON MIRNAS. GENETIC VARIANCE OF SOME MIRNA GENES, ESPECIALLY MIR-499, MIGHT PREDISPOSE AN INDIVIDUAL TO RA DEVELOPMENT. ADDITIONALLY, ALTERED EXPRESSION OF MANY MIRNAS HAS BEEN DISCOVERED IN SEVERAL CELLS, TISSUES AND BODY FLUIDS IN PATIENTS WITH RA. MIRNAS EXPRESSION ALSO DIFFERS DEPENDING ON DISEASE'S STAGE AND ACTIVITY.
