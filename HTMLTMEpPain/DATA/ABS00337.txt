10 2262  37 EPIGENETIC PROFILING IN CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA REVEALS NOVEL METHYLATION TARGETS. CPG ISLAND METHYLATION IS AN EPIGENETIC ALTERATION THAT CONTRIBUTES TO TUMORIGENESIS BY TRANSCRIPTIONAL INACTIVATION OF GENES. 11 3071  47 GENOME-WIDE DNA METHYLATION PROFILING INTEGRATED WITH GENE EXPRESSION PROFILING IDENTIFIES PAX9 AS A NOVEL PROGNOSTIC MARKER IN CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA.
