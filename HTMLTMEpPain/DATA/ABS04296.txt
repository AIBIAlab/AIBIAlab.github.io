1 4296 115 MICRORNA-1 MODULATES CHONDROCYTE PHENOTYPE BY REGULATING FZD7 OF WNT/ BETA-CATENIN SIGNALING PATHWAY. OBJECTIVE: OSTEOARTHRITIS (OA) IS AN INCURABLE JOINT DISEASE CHARACTERIZED BY PRONOUNCED PAIN. 12 3729  46 INHIBITION OF TET1 PREVENTS THE DEVELOPMENT OF OSTEOARTHRITIS AND REVEALS THE 5HMC LANDSCAPE THAT ORCHESTRATES PATHOGENESIS.
