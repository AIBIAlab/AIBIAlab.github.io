16  4082  36 MATERNAL MODIFIERS OF THE INFANT GUT MICROBIOTA: METABOLIC CONSEQUENCES. IN ADDITION TO THIS, RECENT RESEARCH HAS ILLUSTRATED THE IMPORTANCE OF THE ROLE OF INHERENT BIOLOGICAL DIFFERENCES IN LIPID METABOLISM, GLUCOSE METABOLISM, INFLAMMATORY STATES, GENETIC PREDISPOSITIONS AND EPIGENETIC INFLUENCES FOR THE INCREASED RISK. A MULTIFACETED AND HOLISTIC APPROACH TO CVD PREVENTION THAT TAKES INTO CONSIDERATION POPULATION-LEVEL AS WELL AS BIOLOGICAL RISK FACTORS WOULD BE NEEDED TO CONTROL THE BURGEONING CVD EPIDEMIC AMONG INDIANS.	2023
