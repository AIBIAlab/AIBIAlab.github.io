CONCLUSIONS: TAKEN TOGETHER, MIR-34B/C IS A TUMOR SUPPRESSOR MIRNA FREQUENTLY METHYLATED, AND HENCE SILENCED IN CLL. TOGETHER WITH DAPK1 METHYLATION, MIR-34B/C METHYLATION IS IMPLICATED IN THE DISRUPTION OF THE TP53-CENTERED TUMOR SUPPRESSOR NETWORK.
