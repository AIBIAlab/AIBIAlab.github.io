1   131 110 A2B ADENOSINE SIGNALING REPRESSES CIITA TRANSCRIPTION VIA AN EPIGENETIC MECHANISM IN VASCULAR SMOOTH MUSCLE CELLS. VASCULAR SMOOTH MUSCLE CELLS (VSMC), BY EXPRESSING AND PRESENTING MAJOR HISTOCOMPATIBILITY COMPLEX II (MHC II) MOLECULES, HELP RECRUIT T LYMPHOCYTE AND INITIATE THE INFLAMMATORY RESPONSE WITHIN THE VASCULATURE.
