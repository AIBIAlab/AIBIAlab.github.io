OUR STUDY REVEALS AN EPIGENETIC MECHANISM UNDERLYING CXCR3 EXPRESSION AND ALSO SUGGESTS THAT TARGETING THE EXPRESSION OR ACTIVATION OF CXCR3 SIGNALING MAY OFFER NEW THERAPEUTICS FOR NEUROPATHIC PAIN.	2017
