HERE, WE PROVIDE A SYSTEMATIC AND UP-TO-DATE OVERVIEW OF THE CURRENT KNOWLEDGE OF THREE DISTINCT BUT INTERRELATED EPIGENETIC PROCESSES (INCLUDING DNA METHYLATION, HISTONE METHYLATION/ACETYLATION, AND NON-CODING RNAS), IN ATHEROSCLEROTIC PLAQUE DEVELOPMENT AND INSTABILITY.
