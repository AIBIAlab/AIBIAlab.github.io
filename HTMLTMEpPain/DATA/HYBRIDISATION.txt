20  5226  28 PRMT5 RESTRICTS HEPATITIS B VIRUS REPLICATION THROUGH EPIGENETIC REPRESSION OF COVALENTLY CLOSED CIRCULAR DNA TRANSCRIPTION AND INTERFERENCE WITH PREGENOMIC RNA ENCAPSIDATION. CHRONIC HEPATITIS B VIRUS (HBV) INFECTION REMAINS A MAJOR HEALTH PROBLEM WORLDWIDE. THE COVALENTLY CLOSED CIRCULAR DNA (CCCDNA) MINICHROMOSOME, WHICH SERVES AS THE TEMPLATE FOR THE TRANSCRIPTION OF VIRAL RNAS, PLAYS A KEY ROLE IN VIRAL PERSISTENCE. WHILE ACCUMULATING EVIDENCE SUGGESTS THAT CCCDNA TRANSCRIPTION IS REGULATED BY EPIGENETIC MACHINERY, PARTICULARLY THE ACETYLATION OF CCCDNA-BOUND HISTONE 3 (H3) AND H4, THE POTENTIAL CONTRIBUTIONS OF HISTONE METHYLATION AND RELATED HOST FACTORS REMAIN OBSCURE.
