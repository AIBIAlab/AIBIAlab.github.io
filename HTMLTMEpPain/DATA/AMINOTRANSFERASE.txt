BACKGROUND: LIVER FIBROSIS IS AN INDEPENDENT CONTRIBUTOR OF CHRONIC LIVER DISEASES, AND REGRESSING LIVER FIBROSIS IS CONSIDERED A POTENTIAL THERAPEUTIC TARGET FOR CHRONIC LIVER DISEASES.
