DNA METHYLATION IS A FUNDAMENTAL EPIGENETIC MODIFICATION TO REGULATE GENE EXPRESSION. N-MYC DOWNSTREAM-REGULATED GENE (NDRG) 2 IS A CYTOPLASMIC PROTEIN AND PARTICIPATES IN THE PATHOGENESIS OF LIVER FIBROSIS.
