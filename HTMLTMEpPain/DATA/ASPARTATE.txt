CONCLUSION: SORAFENIB EXERTS SIGNIFICANT ANTI-FIBROTIC EFFECTS THROUGH MEDIATING CROTONYLATION-REGULATED ENZYMES AND PROTEIN CROTONYLATION IN FIBROTIC RATS.	2022
