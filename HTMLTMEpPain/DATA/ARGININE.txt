9  5226 35 PRMT5 RESTRICTS HEPATITIS B VIRUS REPLICATION THROUGH EPIGENETIC REPRESSION OF COVALENTLY CLOSED CIRCULAR DNA TRANSCRIPTION AND INTERFERENCE WITH PREGENOMIC RNA ENCAPSIDATION. CHRONIC HEPATITIS B VIRUS (HBV) INFECTION REMAINS A MAJOR HEALTH PROBLEM WORLDWIDE. THE COVALENTLY CLOSED CIRCULAR DNA (CCCDNA) MINICHROMOSOME, WHICH SERVES AS THE TEMPLATE FOR THE TRANSCRIPTION OF VIRAL RNAS, PLAYS A KEY ROLE IN VIRAL PERSISTENCE. CONCLUSION: PRMT5 RESTRICTS HBV REPLICATION THROUGH A TWO-PART MECHANISM INCLUDING EPIGENETIC SUPPRESSION OF CCCDNA TRANSCRIPTION AND INTERFERENCE WITH PREGENOMIC RNA ENCAPSIDATION; THESE FINDINGS IMPROVE THE UNDERSTANDING OF EPIGENETIC REGULATION OF HBV TRANSCRIPTION AND HOST-HBV INTERACTION, THUS PROVIDING NEW INSIGHTS INTO TARGETED THERAPEUTIC INTERVENTION.
