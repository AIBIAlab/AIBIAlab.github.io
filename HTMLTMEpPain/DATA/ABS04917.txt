11 5214  36 PRETERM BEHAVIORAL EPIGENETICS: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW. BEHAVIORAL EPIGENETICS IS REVEALING NEW PATHWAYS THAT LEAD INDIVIDUALS FROM EARLY ADVERSITY EXPOSURES TO LATER-IN-LIFE DETRIMENTAL OUTCOMES. PRETERM BIRTH CONSTITUTES ONE OF THE MAJOR ADVERSE EVENTS IN HUMAN DEVELOPMENT.
