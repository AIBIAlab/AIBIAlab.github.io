20 3703  27 INFLAMMATORY SIGNALLING AS MEDIATOR OF EPIGENETIC MODULATION IN TISSUE-SPECIFIC CHRONIC INFLAMMATION. DESPITE EXTENSIVE RESEARCH INTO THESE PROCESSES, THE LINK BETWEEN CELLULAR SIGNALLING AND EPIGENETIC MODULATION REMAINS POORLY UNDERSTOOD.
