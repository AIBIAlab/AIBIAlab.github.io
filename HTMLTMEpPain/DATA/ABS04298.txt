20 2508  23 EPIGENETICS AND OSTEOARTHRITIS. OSTEOARTHRITIS (OA) IS THE MOST COMMON FORM OF JOINT DISEASE AND THE LEADING CAUSE OF CHRONIC DISABILITY IN MIDDLE-AGED AND OLDER POPULATIONS. THE DEVELOPMENT OF DISEASE-MODIFYING THERAPY FOR OA CURRENTLY FACES MAJOR OBSTACLES LARGELY BECAUSE THE REGULATORY MECHANISMS FOR THE FUNCTION OF JOINT TISSUE CELLS REMAIN UNCLEAR.
