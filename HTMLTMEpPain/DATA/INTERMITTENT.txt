7  2489  37 EPIGENETICALLY MODIFIED NUCLEOTIDES IN CHRONIC HEROIN AND COCAINE TREATED MICE. 11  3410  43 HOXA5 UNDERGOES DYNAMIC DNA METHYLATION AND TRANSCRIPTIONAL REPRESSION IN THE ADIPOSE TISSUE OF MICE EXPOSED TO HIGH-FAT DIET.
